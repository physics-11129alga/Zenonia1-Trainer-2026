ZENONIA is a 2009 action role-playing game developed by Gamevil. It debuted on iOS on May 24, 2009, and later released on Android. The game follows Regret through a fantasy world filled with real-time combat, quests, exploration, equipment, and character progression.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ZENONIA 1**. A PC trainer for ZENONIA that modifies health, skill resources, experience, gold, combat damage, and character progression.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with Warrior, Paladin, or Assassin builds, reduce grinding, test the skill system, revisit the story, or customize the difficulty of ZENONIA.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore towns, fields, caves, and dungeons in real time. Move through each area, speak with NPCs, inspect objects, and follow quest markers or journal objectives. Save before entering unfamiliar areas or making major progression changes.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the inventory to review equipment, weapons, armor, talismans, consumables, and quest items. Equip upgrades appropriate to the chosen class, sell unwanted gear for gold, and keep healing items available for difficult encounters. Enable inventory-related options only after the relevant item is visible in the game.

### World & Environment

ZENONIA combines story missions with optional errands, enemy-filled areas, towns, merchants, and dungeons. Progress through the main quest while exploring side content, collecting equipment, and making choices that influence the game's good and evil alignment path.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is real-time and uses normal attacks, class abilities, consumables, and skill-tree upgrades. Warrior, Paladin, and Assassin each provide a different style of play. Activate health or SP options before difficult fights, and disable damage-related options when you want normal enemy encounters and boss mechanics.
